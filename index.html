What is MacFrame?

MacFrame is our IBM Mainframe emulator for macOS. It allows you to run MVS 3.8J on your Mac with a single click, featuring native Apple Silicon support, a built-in 3270 terminal, and DASD browser. It's free for personal and educational use.

What is Business Profiler?

Business Profiler is a free Claude Code plugin for cybersecurity threat intelligence and sales targeting. It runs recon against a target domain, maps threat actors via MITRE ATT&CK, checks regulatory exposure, and generates PDF reports. The sales-targeting skill turns that data into account strategy with a 38-service catalog and engagement plans. Available on GitHub.
