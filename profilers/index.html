What it does

🎯

Threat profiling

Target-specific risk and exposure analysis mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Sector-specific threat actors, TTPs, and breach history.

🌐

Attack surface recon

Subdomains, cloud detection, email security audit, SSL/TLS, tech stack fingerprinting, WHOIS. 8 scripts running in parallel.

💰

Sales targeting

Turn threat data into account strategy. 38-service catalog, financial analysis, regulatory pressure, contact intelligence, engagement plans.

📋

Regulatory analysis

NIS2, DORA, GDPR, CRA, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOC2, CMMC. Deadlines, penalties, and compliance gap assessment per sector and country.

📄

PDF reports

Markdown and PDF output with Mermaid diagrams for org charts and attack surface maps. Styled with the Peach Studio theme.

🔓

Free data sources

No API keys needed. Uses crt.sh, MITRE ATT&CK STIX, XposedOrNot, CISA KEV, Wikidata, RDAP, and Claude's WebSearch.

7 Skills

Full Profile

/business-profiler:full-profile

The complete pipeline. Recon, business intel, threat actors, regulatory context, OT/ICS assessment, 15-20 page PDF report.

Threat Profile

/business-profiler:threat-profile

Threat-focused. Lightweight recon with deep threat actor analysis, breach intel, incident research, and regulatory context.

Attack Surface

/business-profiler:attack-surface

Infrastructure recon only. Subdomains, cloud providers, email security, SSL/TLS, tech stack, no threat actor analysis.

Threat Actors

/business-profiler:threat-actors

MITRE ATT&CK lookup by sector, actor name, or country. Returns actor profiles with TTPs and targeting relevance.

OT/ICS Assessment

/business-profiler:ot-ics-assessment

Industrial control systems focus. ICS subdomain patterns, OT threat actors, Purdue model mapping, ICS malware intel.

Incident Lookup

/business-profiler:incident-lookup

WebSearch-based breach and incident research. Timeline of incidents with dates, actors, impact, and source URLs.

Sales Targeting

/business-profiler:sales-targeting

6-part account strategy report. Financial analysis, 38-service catalog with pricing, contact intelligence, engagement sequence.
